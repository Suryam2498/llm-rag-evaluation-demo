# Billing

Bills are issued on the 5th of each month for the previous month's consumption.

Payment is due within 15 days of the bill date. After this 15-day grace period, a late fee of 2% of the outstanding amount is added.

Customers can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking through the app or the portal.

Customers who enable autopay receive an e-bill by email and an SMS reminder three days before the due date.
